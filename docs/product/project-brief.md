# Project Brief

## Name
Implicit Exchange (ImplicitEx)

## What It Is
The simple, affordable, self-custodial stablecoin payment workspace for
independent operators who need more than a wallet or free invoice link, but do
not need a finance department.

## Strategic Thesis
The stablecoin transfer itself is commoditized. Coin Card is the persistent
payment identity inside a workflow that lets an operator request, receive,
match, preserve, find, and export a payment. Route lineage and portable
evidence support the workflow; they are not assumed to be the business alone.

## Delivery Strategy
- Primary: Validate a controlled, manually issued Polygon USDC Coin Card pilot.
- Commercial: Test a payee-side independent-operator workflow at $9–$49 per
  month, fixed managed-request pricing, limited assisted onboarding, and the
  deployed transaction fee as separate hypotheses.
- Trust: Ratify and use the existing signed-manifest, lifecycle, registry,
  execution-authorization, and transaction-evidence architecture.
- Product: Complete identity → request → receive → match → evidence → search
  and export before horizontal asset or network expansion. Build portal
  capability through progressive disclosure over shared objects rather than
  adding disconnected panels.
- V2: Pass the account-and-data Gate 0, then build only fixed requests,
  transaction-hash binding, a minimal confirmation watcher, evidence, basic
  search, and CSV. External or unobserved payments remain suggested matches.
- Later: Add one V2 Expansion capability at a time. V3 selects at most one
  workflow and one access experiment after customer evidence identifies the
  blockers.

## Priority Statement
Reach at least $25,000 in annual gross revenue through a repeatable,
supportable workflow. The expected target is approximately 50–150 consistently
paying customers at the middle or upper end of the proposed price range or
under a mixed revenue model; a $9-only plan would require 232 customers.

Desktop software, hardware-wallet expansion, and agent financial
infrastructure are not current priorities. They must not displace customer
discovery, workflow depth, security review, or paid validation.
