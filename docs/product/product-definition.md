# Product Definition

## Product Type
An affordable, self-custodial stablecoin payment workspace for independent
operators, beginning with a controlled Polygon USDC Coin Card pilot.

## Strategic Direction
ImplicitEx turns wallet movements into named, requested, matched, searchable,
and exportable business events. Coin Card supplies persistent payment identity;
signed requests, lifecycle status, request-to-transaction evidence, simple
exception detection, history, and export form the first commercial workflow.

The initial buyer hypothesis is the recipient/operator: a freelancer,
consultant, creator, developer, micro-agency, or one-person online business
already receiving stablecoins, occasionally paying collaborators, and needing
more than a wallet or free invoice link but less than an enterprise finance
suite. The operating doctrine is vertical workflow depth before additional
assets or networks.

The portal follows progressive disclosure over shared business-profile,
contact, Coin Card, request, transfer, evidence, template, schedule, and
exception objects. Customization means task views, filters, columns, and
curated presentation—not hidden execution facts, arbitrary CSS, or competing
records of payment status.

## Scope (Current)
Complete and validate a manually issued, time-limited Coin Card pilot without
taking custody. Preserve the transfer engine, ratify the existing signed-record
trust stack, close the production wallet-control gate, and test placement,
genuine third-party payments, evidence value, and explicit pricing.

## Scope (Later)
A lightweight paid workspace begins only after an account-and-data Gate 0
covers authentication, recovery, tenant isolation, authorization, retention,
deletion, backups, administrative access, audit logs, privacy, PII, and incident
response.

V2 Core contains fixed signed requests, lifecycle, recorded transaction
submission, a minimal confirmation watcher, request-to-transaction evidence,
exact/unmatched/failed/expired/cancelled outcomes, basic search, and CSV.
Only a request-launched transaction whose hash and verified event are durably
bound may be called authoritatively matched; external or incompletely observed
transfers remain operator-reviewed suggestions.

Contacts, templates, reminders, partial or excessive payments, duplicate
detection, analytics, themes, identity claims, and advanced exceptions are
one-at-a-time V2 Expansion candidates. V3 selects at most one workflow and one
access experiment after V2 evidence identifies the blockers.

The current interface limits transfers to 250 USDC, making 2.50 USDC the
maximum reachable fee. The deployed contract charges 1% without an absolute
fee cap; a future 10 USDC contract ceiling is policy, not deployed behavior.
The fee remains an unproven pricing path, not the business model.

Desktop software and agent financial infrastructure remain optional strategic
initiatives, not current roadmap priorities.
