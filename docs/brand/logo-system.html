Brand identity

A direct, technical mark for wallet-to-wallet transfer.

The ImplicitEx identity is built from a compact lettermark and a geometric wordmark. On dark surfaces, both marks are white. On light surfaces, both marks are black.

Color Use

The mark is monochrome only. The lettermark and wordmark must always share the same contrast rule against their surface.

Relationship Rules

The lettermark and wordmark should behave like one optical unit. Use fixed ratios for production lockups instead of resizing each piece independently.

Inline Header Use a 200 px wordmark with a 29 px lettermark and an 11-12 px gap. This matches the combined asset more closely than a 25 px lettermark beside a 200 px wordmark.
Stacked / Centered Use the same optical relationship at larger scale: the lettermark should read as a distinct symbol, not a small icon. Keep the wordmark dominant in width and the lettermark dominant in height.
Small Spaces Below 140 px wordmark width, drop the wordmark and use the lettermark alone. Do not squeeze the wordmark or shrink the lettermark until the lockup loses hierarchy.
Alignment Align the lettermark optically to the wordmark centerline. Do not baseline-align the lettermark to the lower edge of the wordmark; the symbol should sit centered as a companion mark.
Color The lettermark and wordmark must always share the same contrast rule: white artwork on dark surfaces, black artwork on light surfaces.

Primary Lockups

Use the combined mark in website headers and product surfaces. Use the wordmark when horizontal space is available, and the lettermark when space is constrained.

Usage

The mark should feel precise and operational, not decorative. Keep the system quiet, high contrast, and aligned to the product interface.

Color

Use the lettermark and wordmark in white on dark surfaces and black on light surfaces. Avoid gradients, shadows, outlines, or extra color inside the mark.

Spacing

Leave at least one lettermark width around the combined mark when it appears as a standalone identity element.

Header

Use the lettermark plus wordmark in desktop headers. On narrow mobile screens, the lettermark can stand alone.

Minimum

Do not force the wordmark below 140 px wide. Use the lettermark alone for small stamps and icons, but omit it from wordmark lockups when it would render below 24 px or lose clarity.
