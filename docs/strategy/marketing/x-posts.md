### Option 1 — Verification Philosophy (DRAFT)

> The internet spent 30 years teaching us to trust intermediaries.
>
> Crypto introduced a different idea:
>
> Verify instead of trust.
>
> Verify the address.
> Verify the transaction.
> Verify the contract.
> Verify the fee.
>
> The goal isn't to eliminate trust.
>
> The goal is to reduce the amount of trust required.
