Getting started

What is IX ID?

IX ID gives you a permanent identity for receiving payments on the Polygon network. Instead of sharing a long wallet address, you share a handle — like maria.ixid.me. The handle stays the same even if you change which wallet you receive payments at.

IX ID is not a wallet and does not hold money. It is an identity layer — it tells others where to send funds, and you can update that destination without anyone needing to update the address they have for you.

Creating an IX ID

  1. Go to app.ixid.me/register.
  2. Enter your email address and a password (at least 12 characters).
  3. Click Create account. A verification email will be sent.
  4. Open the email and click the verification link. You will return to IX ID automatically.
  5. Choose your IX ID handle (3–30 lowercase letters, numbers, or hyphens).
  6. Click Claim IX ID. Your handle is now registered.

Verifying your email

After creating an account, IX ID sends a verification link to your email. Click it to confirm ownership. If the link expires, return to app.ixid.me/register, sign in, and click Resend email.

Choosing a handle

Your handle must be 3–30 characters, lowercase letters, numbers, and single hyphens only. It cannot start or end with a hyphen. Certain common words are reserved.

Your handle is permanent and cannot be changed after registration. Choose something you are comfortable using as your public identity.

Connecting a wallet In development

After claiming your handle, you will be able to connect a Polygon-compatible wallet to publish your payment destination. Wallet connection is in the current workspace, and publishing routes to the public identity page is in development.

Using your IX ID

Sharing your IX ID

Your public IX ID address is yourhandle.ixid.me. Share it wherever you want to be findable for payments — your email signature, website, or social profiles.

Your public identity page

Anyone can visit yourhandle.ixid.me and see your registered identity information. Currently the page shows verified domain information where present. Payment functionality will be shown there once your payment route is published.

Receiving payments In development

Once payment routes are live, USDC sent to your IX ID will arrive directly in your registered wallet. IX ID does not hold or forward the funds — they travel directly from the sender's wallet to yours on the Polygon network.

Updating your payment wallet In development

When the payment route feature is live, you will be able to update the wallet your IX ID points to. Your handle stays the same; only the destination changes. Anyone who already has your handle will automatically reach your new wallet.

Profile fields In development

Display name, bio, website, avatar, and banner are planned for a future update.

Signing out

Click Sign out in your workspace. Because IX ID uses in-memory session storage, you are also signed out automatically when you close the browser tab.

Troubleshooting

Verification link expired

Return to app.ixid.me/register, sign in, and click Resend email. Wait at least 60 seconds between resend attempts.

Verification email not received

Check your spam or junk folder. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, request a resend. Make sure your email address was entered correctly.

Handle unavailable

The handle is either already taken or reserved. Try a different one. Handles that are taken are not disclosed — the claim is simply rejected.

Wallet won't connect

Make sure your browser wallet extension (such as MetaMask) is installed and unlocked. Approve the connection request when prompted. If it fails, refresh the page and try again.

Wrong network

IX ID uses the Polygon network (Chain ID 137). If your wallet is on a different network, switch to Polygon and try again.
