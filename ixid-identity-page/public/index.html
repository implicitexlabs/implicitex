How it works

  1. Create an IX ID. Choose a handle. It becomes the identity you share — not a wallet address.
  2. Connect your wallet. Link any Polygon-compatible wallet as your current payment destination. You stay in full control of it.
  3. Get paid. Visitors open your IX ID page, enter an amount, and confirm in their own wallet. USDC arrives directly in yours — IX ID never touches it.

IX ID does not hold your money

IX ID is a permanent identity layer, not a bank. When someone pays you, the transaction goes directly from their wallet to yours on the Polygon network. IX ID resolves the destination — it does not touch the funds.

Your wallet stays yours at all times. IX ID cannot move money on your behalf.
