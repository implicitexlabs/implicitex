What IX ID does

IX ID is a payment identity service. It gives you a permanent, human-readable handle that others can use to find your current payment destination on the Polygon network. IX ID resolves your handle to your registered wallet address and constructs payment requests. It does not process, hold, or transfer funds.

What IX ID does not do

  • IX ID is not a bank and does not hold money.
  • IX ID is not a custodial wallet. We do not have access to your funds or private keys.
  • IX ID does not execute blockchain transactions. Transactions are authorized and submitted by the user's own wallet software.
  • IX ID does not guarantee that registered wallet addresses are correct. You are solely responsible for verifying that the wallet address you register is one you control.

Accounts and handles

You must provide a valid email address to create an account. Handles are 3–30 characters, lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only. Certain handles are reserved. One handle is issued per verified email address at this time.

Your handle is your public payment identity and is visible to anyone. Choose a handle you are comfortable sharing publicly. Handles cannot be changed after registration.

Your wallet and payment route

The wallet address you connect becomes your registered payment destination, visible to anyone who visits your IX ID page. The wallet itself remains entirely under your control — IX ID has no access to it.

You may update the wallet that receives payments. When you do, the previous route is deactivated and the new one becomes active. Payments directed to your IX ID after the change go to the new wallet.

You are solely responsible for verifying that you control any wallet address you register. IX ID does not verify wallet ownership beyond your declaration at this time. If you register an address you do not control, payments directed to your IX ID will go to the wrong destination and cannot be recovered.

Sending payments through IX ID

When you initiate a payment from an IX ID page, you are constructing a transaction submitted through your own wallet software. You are responsible for confirming the correct handle, wallet address, amount, and network (Polygon) before approving the transaction in your wallet.

Blockchain transactions are generally irreversible once submitted. IX ID cannot reverse, cancel, or recover a completed transaction.

Transaction fees

IX ID itself does not charge a fee for registrations or identity lookups. Payment services accessed when completing a transaction through IX ID may display their own fees before you authorize the transaction. Review the fee disclosure shown to you at the time of payment before confirming.

Network gas fees are separate, paid in POL (the Polygon network token), and are not collected by IX ID.
