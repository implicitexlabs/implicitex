## Change 4 — Add Coin Card section

**Location:** Insert as a new section after "Information You May Provide" and before "Wallet and Blockchain Information."

**Draft:**

### Coin Card — Account and Purchase Information

If you purchase a Coin Card, ImplicitEx collects and stores the following information in order to create and maintain your Coin Card, process your payment, and provide customer support:

**Identity and account information:**
- Email address
- Email verification status
- The display name and public handle you choose for your Coin Card

**Wallet information:**
- The wallet address you associate with your Coin Card at the time of purchase

This wallet address is stored in the ImplicitEx registry and is publicly associated with your Coin Card handle. It is not a private key and does not grant ImplicitEx any access to or control over your wallet.

**Payment and billing information:**
- Purchase date, amount, and currency
- Payment method type and last four digits (retained by our payment processor; ImplicitEx stores a payment processor customer identifier, not your full card number)
- Refund status, refund request date, and refund approval date, if applicable

ImplicitEx uses a third-party payment processor to handle payment transactions. Your full payment card details are processed and stored by that processor under their own privacy policy and security standards; ImplicitEx does not receive or store your full card number.

**Account history and fraud prevention records:**
- Registry and lifecycle timestamps for your Coin Card (creation date, deactivation date, and similar operational records)
- Records related to refund requests, eligibility reviews, and outcomes
- Internal notes associated with fraud-prevention review, where applicable

**Support communications:**
- Emails, messages, or other communications you send to support@implicitex.com in connection with your Coin Card

## What this policy does not claim to collect

The following are deliberately absent because Coin Card does not actually collect them:

- Device fingerprints
- Precise geolocation or GPS data
- Biometric information
- Government-issued identity documents
- Full payment card numbers (processed by payment processor only)
- Browser history or behavioral profiles beyond basic server logs
- Social connections or third-party account data

Do not add these to the policy unless and until the system genuinely collects them.
