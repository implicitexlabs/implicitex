## 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your Coin Card, you may request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase date.

Eligible refunds return the full amount you paid, including any applicable refundable taxes, to your original payment method. ImplicitEx absorbs ordinary payment-processing costs; you receive back exactly what you paid.

---

## Eligibility

The 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee applies to the **first Coin Card purchase associated with your ImplicitEx account**. An account is eligible for no more than one refund under this guarantee.

Your refund request must be submitted within 30 days of the date your purchase was completed.

This limitation does not affect any refund or remedy required by applicable law, nor does it prevent ImplicitEx from granting another refund at its discretion.

---

## How to Request a Refund

Contact ImplicitEx at support@implicitex.com with your purchase confirmation and the email address associated with your account.

We will normally review and respond to eligible refund requests within three business days. Once approved and submitted, the time required for the refund to appear in your account depends on your payment provider and financial institution.

---

## What Happens After a Refund Is Approved

Refund eligibility is confirmed before any action is taken on your account. The following occurs as part of the approved refund transaction:

**Your Coin Card is deactivated.** Once a refund is approved and initiated, your Coin Card no longer routes payments and is no longer displayed as active on the ImplicitEx platform.

**Your reserved handle enters a recovery period.** Your handle is not immediately returned to the public registry. During the recovery period, no other person can register it. If you change your mind, you may repurchase the same handle during this time.

**After the recovery period, the handle becomes available to others.** Once the recovery period ends, your handle returns to the public registry and may be registered by a new customer.

A refund request alone does not deactivate your Coin Card. Deactivation occurs only after ImplicitEx confirms eligibility and approves the refund.

## Data After Deactivation

After your Coin Card is deactivated, ImplicitEx retains records related to your purchase for accounting, fraud prevention, and legal compliance purposes. Please see the [Privacy Policy] for details on what data is collected and how it is used.

## Limitations

**First purchase per account.** The 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee applies to your first Coin Card purchase. Subsequent purchases are not automatically covered by this guarantee.

**One guarantee refund per account.** An account that has received a refund under this guarantee is not eligible for an additional guarantee refund on a future purchase.

**Good-faith use required.** This guarantee is intended for customers who genuinely try Coin Card and are unsatisfied. Repeated or manipulative refund requests — including purchasing, refunding, and repurchasing the same handle — may be reviewed, and future purchases may be declined at ImplicitEx's discretion.

**Statutory rights preserved.** Nothing in this policy limits any right or remedy available to you under applicable consumer protection law.
