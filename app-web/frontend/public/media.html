Platform Overview

ImplicitEx is a non-custodial platform for wallet-to-wallet USDC transfers on Polygon. Users connect their own wallet, enter a recipient address and amount, review the fee and total debit, and execute the transfer through the ImplicitEx smart contract. ImplicitEx does not hold private keys, does not custody user assets, and does not act as a bank, broker, or exchange.

The platform charges a 1% fee added on top of the transfer amount. The recipient receives the full amount entered. All fee and total debit figures are displayed before the user takes any wallet action. No hidden spreads. No vague service charges.

ImplicitEx is a product of Aden Media Group LLC, a software company building practical, transparent tools for digital commerce and everyday users.

Platform Description

The following text may be used as-is or adapted when describing ImplicitEx in an article, post, or broadcast segment.

ImplicitEx is a non-custodial platform that lets users send USDC directly between wallets using a smart contract on Polygon. The platform charges a 1% fee on each transfer and displays the full fee breakdown before any wallet action is taken. ImplicitEx does not hold user funds at any point in the transaction. Transfers are executed on-chain and are irreversible once confirmed. ImplicitEx launched in soft-launch mode on Polygon mainnet in June 2026 with a 250 USDC per-transfer cap.

Key Messages

  • Non-custodial by design. ImplicitEx never holds user funds. The smart contract routes USDC between wallets without any platform custody step.
  • Transparent fee structure. The 1% fee is shown explicitly before execution — no hidden spreads, no vague service charges.
  • On-chain verifiable. The contract is source-verified and the transaction record is publicly accessible on Polygonscan.
  • Built for everyday users. The interface is designed around plain-language status messages and deliberate confirmation at each step.

What ImplicitEx Is Not

ImplicitEx is not a bank, broker, exchange, custodian, wallet provider, or money services business. It does not hold private keys, does not recover funds sent to wrong addresses, and does not provide financial, legal, or tax advice. See the Legal page for the full scope of platform limitations.
