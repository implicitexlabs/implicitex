Command

What V1 must prove

01

Trustworthy route

A payer can verify the current signed route, wallet-control evidence, lifecycle state, and transaction-time record.

02

Real use

Qualified recipients place controlled cards and receive genuine third-party payments—not demos or founder-created traffic.

03

Operable pilot

Issuance, suspension, revocation, expiry, evidence, incidents, and support work without unsafe improvisation.

Execution sequence

Work in this order

  1. 1

    Lock the governing decisions

    Ratify the signed-record stack, namespace policy, minimal V1 data boundary, custody tripwires, validity period, and reverification interval.

  2. 2

    Close production-security dependencies

    Resolve Gate 1A, complete the structured security review, harden administrative accounts, and establish the served-content integrity tripwire.

  3. 3

    Complete the controlled credential path

    Verify wallet control, sign the manifest, publish lifecycle state, fail closed when authority is unavailable, and preserve the governing route in the evidence packet.

  4. 4

    Prepare pilot operations

    Approve the cohort manually; rehearse issuance, support, suspension, revocation, expiry, compromise, and incident procedures; obtain counsel’s screening determination before the first genuine payment.

  5. 5

    Run the evidence program

    Make qualified offers, place cards, observe genuine third-party payments, retain evidence, and record where the workflow helped or failed.

  6. 6

    Make the gate decision

    Advance only if the binding gates pass and the working thresholds justify V2. Otherwise pause, narrow, or stop according to the recorded evidence.

Scope control

What belongs in V1

Included

  • Manually approved, time-limited pilot entitlements
  • Hosted Coin Card route with durable card ID and revocable handle
  • Production wallet-control verification
  • Signed manifest and independently usable verifier
  • Lifecycle, revocation, supersession, and degraded unavailable state
  • Static iframe or hosted link
  • Polygon USDC only
  • Current transfer execution, receipt, and proof packet
  • Issuance, revocation, incident, and support runbooks

Excluded until a later gate

  • Billing and automatic renewal
  • Public self-service issuance or public Free tier
  • Analytics beyond operating evidence
  • Additional assets or chains
  • Directory or discovery
  • On-ramp or off-ramp
  • Embedded wallets
  • Arbitrary customization
  • Automatic or recurring payment pulls

Decision rules

Binding gates and working thresholds

Binding gates

Failure blocks release regardless of customer interest.

  • Production security and dependency closure
  • Custody and authority boundary
  • Signed-record and evidence integrity
  • Fail-closed lifecycle behavior
  • Legal and screening determination for the actual V1 flow
  • No unresolved critical or high-severity finding

Working evidence thresholds

These support the next decision; they are not market validation.

  • 10 controlled cards placed
  • 3 genuine third-party payments
  • 5 placements from no more than 50 qualified offers
  • Evidence that recipients share and reuse the credential
  • Evidence that the route or proof distinction matters
Stop rule. If fewer than five qualified prospects place a card after 50 direct offers, stop feature expansion and re-interview the segment. If placed cards do not receive genuine payments, investigate the actual use case before billing.

Master boundary

Authority ImplicitEx does not take

ImplicitEx does not receive, hold, independently direct, guarantee, insure, lend, convert, or recover customer funds.

The user controls the wallet and authorizes the transaction. Any feature that would introduce key control, balances, independent initiation, release control, conversion, yield, credit, or principal-loss reimbursement stops for written legal and architectural review.
