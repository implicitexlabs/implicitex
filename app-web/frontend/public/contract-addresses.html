How to Verify

Before confirming any transfer, the Transfer Portal displays a preview that includes the contract address, USDC token address, network, recipient, fee, and total. Compare these values against the addresses on this page.

To verify independently on Polygonscan:

  • Open the ImplicitEx contract on Polygonscan using the link above.
  • Check that the contract is source-verified (green checkmark on the Contract tab).
  • Review recent transactions to confirm transfer activity matches expected behavior.
  • The contract source code is published and verifiable on-chain.

The Verification page includes a confirmed on-chain transaction record as additional proof of operation.

What Is Not Listed Here

  • The treasury address is not published on this page.
  • Testnet addresses are internal and not for public use.
  • Do not send funds to any address not listed on this page or shown in the Transfer Portal preview.
