What Each Wallet Prompt Does

ImplicitEx requires two wallet confirmations to execute a transfer. They are separate steps with different effects.

The first prompt asks your wallet to authorize the ImplicitEx contract to transfer a specific USDC amount on your behalf. The authorization is limited to the exact total debit for that transfer — not an unlimited approval. Authorizing alone does not move any funds.

The second prompt executes the transfer. When confirmed, the contract sends the transfer amount to the recipient and the platform fee to the treasury in a single on-chain transaction.

If your wallet security provider shows a warning that the contract is untrusted, that reflects the contract's age and transaction history, not a finding of malicious behavior. The contract address in the warning matches the address listed above.

To verify: compare the contract address shown in your wallet warning with the contract address published on this page. If they match, you are interacting with the same contract documented in the verification record above. The source code is publicly available on Polygonscan. See the FAQ for more detail.
