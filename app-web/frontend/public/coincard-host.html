This transfer required no exchange, no conversion, and no custodial account. The sender held USDC on Polygon, the recipient published a verified Coin Card, and the transfer settled directly between wallets in a single transaction.

The on-chain record is permanent and independently verifiable. No fee was collected by any exchange. The only cost was the 1% ImplicitEx protocol fee and a negligible Polygon gas amount — typically under one cent.

Anyone can verify the recipient address on the ImplicitEx registry before initiating a transfer. The Coin Card is a signed record, not a request. It confirms the destination. The sender decides whether to act.

Further transfers to the same recipient can be initiated at any time without re-entering the address. The Coin Card persists until revoked by the card holder through the ImplicitEx registry.
