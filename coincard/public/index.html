Questions

Do I need a website?

No. Your Coin Card lives at coincard.click/yourname. Share that URL in your bio, your emails, your livestream description — anywhere.

What does the person sending me money need?

A crypto wallet that supports Polygon. MetaMask works. Coinbase Wallet works. Most modern wallets work. They don't need to know your wallet address — that's what the Coin Card handles.

What is USDC?

USDC is a digital dollar. One USDC equals one US dollar. It moves on the Polygon network, which keeps fees very low — usually a fraction of a cent.

What fee do you charge?

ImplicitEx charges 1% on each transfer. Coin Card transactions are capped at 250 USDC, so the maximum fee per transaction is $2.50. Your annual Coin Card fee covers hosting, wallet management, and support — it's separate from the transfer fee.

Do you hold my money?

No. Funds go directly from the sender's wallet to yours. ImplicitEx never holds, stores, or has access to your USDC. We are non-custodial by design.

What if I change my wallet?

Sign in to your account and update your wallet address. Your Coin Card URL stays the same. Everyone who has your link will automatically send to your new wallet.

What if I stop using it?

If you don't renew at the end of the year, your Coin Card is deactivated after a grace period and your username becomes available again. You can always renew before that happens to keep your address.

Who makes Coin Card?

Coin Card is an ImplicitEx product. ImplicitEx builds non-custodial USDC transfer infrastructure on Polygon.
